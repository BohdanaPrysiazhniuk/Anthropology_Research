**Project Overview**

This repository contains the research presentation design for a field study on 16.coffee (Kyiv). The design work translates complex qualitative data and user behaviors into clean, scannable visual artifacts and editorial-grade presentations.  

The goal of the design was to honor the minimal, community-centric aesthetic of the cafe while maintaining high clarity for academic and professional audiences.

**Design Specifications**

_Style & Aesthetics_

Editorial & Minimalist: Grounded in functional Swiss/International Style—prioritizing typography, structured grid systems, and negative space to make complex qualitative findings effortlessly readable.  

Color Palette: Muted earth tones combined with crisp monochrome contrast to reflect the physical interior of the coffee shop and create visual hierarchy across data categories.  

Information Design: Heavy use of structured callouts to illustrate client journeys and physical touchpoints.

_Tools & Technologies_

Figma: Core research planning tool used for mapping out research questions, designing the methodological framework, creating timeline/deadline schedules.   

Canva: Primary slide deck tool used for building presentation templates, visual storytelling, layout design, final deck formatting, and PDF export optimization.
